    1. Navigate to the Kitchen Display (KDS) screen.

    **KDS-04: Sound alert on new order**
    1. Ensure the mute toggle button is visible in the KDS header.

    4. Expected: a chime plays within 1-2 seconds of the order arriving.
    5. Click the mute toggle — button changes to "Muted / Sunet oprit".
    6. Create another order.
    7. Expected: NO sound plays. Visual KDS update still happens normally.

    **KDS-05: Bump button advances ticket**
    1. On the KDS screen, find a ticket in the "Queue" or "Active" column.
    2. Click the bump button (the primary action button on the ticket, typically "Mark Ready" or "Complete").
