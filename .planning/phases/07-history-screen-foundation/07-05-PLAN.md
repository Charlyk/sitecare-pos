surface into an archive view: no timeline, no mutating controls, no thermal ticket, a
history-flavored back label, and a minimal totals card where the itemized receipt would be.

screen. D-08 scopes Phase 7 to the `AdminOrder` fields already in hand; the `getOrder(id)` call that
fills in items/address/prep belongs to the NEW detail-view phase, which will extend THIS same
`readOnly` surface rather than replace it.

Output: modified `src/screen-detail.jsx` and extended `src/__tests__/screen-detail.test.jsx`.
</objective>
