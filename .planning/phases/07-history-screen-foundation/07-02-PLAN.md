<assumption_delta_decision>
## Assumption-Delta — pluralization of "order detail"

**Signal:** `pluralization` (singular → plural). `openOrder()` / `screen: 'detail'` were built when
there was exactly ONE kind of order detail (a live kitchen order, opened from `OrdersScreen`,
editable, back-to-orders). Phase 7 introduces a second context: a finished archived order, opened
from History, read-only, back-to-history.

**The noun that is now primary:** *order detail route* — generalized over `{ order source, data
shape, capability set, return destination }`. The shipped `'detail'` is now one variant of that
general noun, not the noun itself.

**Decision: `add-alongside`** — introduce `openHistoryOrder()` / `screen: 'history-detail'` as a
parallel pair; leave `openOrder()` / `'detail'` byte-identical.

**Rationale, and the promote tradeoff recorded honestly:** the textbook move here is **promote** —
generalize to `openDetail(order, { readOnly, returnTo })`, make that primary, and demote `'detail'`
to the live-order variant. Three project-specific constraints outweigh it:

1. `openOrder()` is live in shipped v1.0 code (`app.jsx:224`, `screen-orders.jsx`) with the whole
   Orders flow behind it. Promoting means rewriting the shipped mutation path to gain a
   generalization Phase 7 does not yet need.
2. CLAUDE.md's pixel-perfect-port constraint and the v1.0 SHIPPED status make an unforced refactor of
   the live order path a poor risk trade for a foundation phase.
3. The two variants' data shapes genuinely differ today: `'detail'` reads a `useOrderDetail(id)`
   *fetch* (kitchen `Order`, with `items[]`), while `'history-detail'` reads an in-memory
   `AdminOrder` with no `items[]`. A single promoted action would have to paper over that.

**Accepted debt, and what would force a promote:** two `open*` actions and two screen values that
render the same component. A **third** entry point into the detail surface, or the moment
`'history-detail'` grows its own `getOrder(id)` fetch and the two variants' data shapes converge
(the NEW detail-view phase per CONTEXT.md `<roadmap_impact>`), makes `add-alongside` the wrong shape
and should trigger the promote.

**Phase 7 does NOT build the detail view** (D-08 defers it). This is therefore a decision **recorded
now and acted on later**: Phase 7 ships the parallel pair; the detail-view phase is the natural place
to revisit promotion once both variants' data shapes are known.
</assumption_delta_decision>
